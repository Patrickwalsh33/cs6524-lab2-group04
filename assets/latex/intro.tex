\section{Introduction}
The aim of this lab was to measure how far away things are using just one phone camera. We first
calibrated each camera to find its intrinsic matrix $K$. Then we filmed a scene while sliding the
camera sideways, so that two frames taken a moment apart work like a pair of stereo cameras. Each
of us used our own phone, giving three cameras and six scenes: camera 01 is an iPhone, and cameras
02 and 03 are Android phones.
